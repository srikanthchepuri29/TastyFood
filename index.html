Help & Support FAQ

After placing an order, you will be taken to the tracking view. There you can see the stages of preparation. To see the rider ride in real-time, go to the Admin Portal (via the top navbar link), find your order, and update the status to "Out for Delivery". The rider motorcyle icon will animate along the route to your house!

Yes, orders can be cancelled easily from the Admin Portal. Select the order status select dropdown and click "Cancelled". The driver will be freed and the status will update on your client tracking screen.

We currently support Cash on Delivery (COD) for testing and safety. This allows you to check out instantly without any real card/UPI transactions.

During checkout, you can drag the marker or click on the map to pin your exact location. The delivery rider will ride directly to this coordinate point.
